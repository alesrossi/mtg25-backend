1. Information We Collect

At Surveyl.top, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and password when you register

  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform, including trades, decks, and tournaments

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in these cases:

  • With other users when you participate in trades or public tournaments

  • To protect the rights and safety of our users

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure password hashing
  • Regular security audits

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your collection data

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and provide core functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
